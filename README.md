# Rome: early access

Builds of Rome for testers. Rome is a sandbox set on a Roman island: towns, roads, markets and the people who live
there, each with a day of their own. These are early 0.x versions: unfinished, and things will change and break.

## Try it

**[Open the simulation viewer](https://cucsijuan.github.io/Rome-releases/)** (in the browser, nothing to install).

It runs the test island live in your browser: three settlements and about 500 people who sleep, work, shop, eat,
go to the baths and the tavern, run errands for their workplace, and trade between towns.

- Drag to move, use the wheel (or pinch) to zoom.
- Click a person to see what they are doing, their needs, household, money and the reasons for their last decision;
  click a building to see its owner, stock and who is inside, and a market's prices.
- Pause and change the speed at the top (space pauses, keys 0 to 4 set the speed). At ×1 a game day lasts 24 minutes.
- Add `?seed=2` (or any number) to the address for another run of the same island.

The version is shown next to the title.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue in this repository: what you saw, the version, the seed if you changed it, and the day and hour shown
at the top.
